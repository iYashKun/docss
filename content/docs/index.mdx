Welcome to your documentation. Use this page to introduce your product and point readers to the information they need first.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/getting-started) explains the first steps for new users.
- Add guides, tutorials, and reference pages under `content/docs/`.

## Organize your docs

Create an `.mdx` file for each page. Folders become URL segments, so `content/docs/guides/accounts.mdx` is available at `/guides/accounts`.
